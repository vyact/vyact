# Vyact Governance

Vyact is an independently maintained open-source project. This document makes project decisions, roles, and paths to greater responsibility explicit.

## Principles

- Keep the project open, transparent, and useful to its users.
- Protect the local-first privacy model and make external data flows clear.
- Prefer decisions that improve long-term maintainability over short-term convenience.
- Welcome contributors regardless of employer or organizational affiliation.
- Do not grant a vendor, sponsor, or investor special control over the project.

Sponsorship, infrastructure credits, or other support do not give the provider preferential influence over technical or community decisions.

## Roles

### Contributors

Anyone who submits code, documentation, translations, testing, design work, issue triage, or constructive community support is a contributor.

### Reviewers

Reviewers are trusted contributors who consistently provide accurate and constructive reviews in an area of the project. Maintainers may invite a contributor to become a reviewer after a sustained record of participation.

### Maintainers

Maintainers can merge changes, manage releases, apply the Code of Conduct, and make project-wide decisions. Vyact currently has one maintainer. The project is actively seeking additional maintainers so responsibility and decision-making can be shared.

A reviewer may be invited to become a maintainer based on sustained contributions, sound technical judgment, respectful collaboration, security awareness, and demonstrated commitment to the project's values. Maintainer appointments and departures will be recorded publicly.

## Decision-Making

Routine changes are decided through issue and pull-request review. Significant changes—such as architecture migrations, data-handling changes, license changes, or community-policy changes—must be proposed publicly and left open for reasonable community feedback before adoption.

Maintainers seek rough consensus. When consensus cannot be reached, the maintainers document the competing considerations and final decision. The current maintainer has final responsibility while the project has only one maintainer, but must explain significant decisions publicly.

## Conflicts of Interest

Contributors and maintainers should disclose relevant financial or organizational interests when participating in a decision. A conflicted maintainer should avoid being the sole decision-maker when another qualified reviewer is available.

## Community Spaces

This governance model applies to the repository and official Vyact community services, including the planned discussion board and real-time chat. All spaces follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Moderation actions should be proportionate, documented privately, and appealable to a maintainer who did not take the original action when one is available.

## Amendments

Changes to this document follow the significant-change process above. As the maintainer group grows, the decision process will be updated to remove the single-maintainer fallback.
