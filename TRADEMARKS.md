Vyact is open-source software licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0). This policy covers the separate use of the **Vyact** name, logo, and official visual brand assets (together, the “Vyact Marks”).

The AGPL-3.0 license grants permission to use, modify, and distribute the software. It does **not** grant permission to use the Vyact Marks except as described in this policy.

## Permitted use

You may use the Vyact Marks without prior permission when doing so is necessary to accurately refer to the unmodified official Vyact project. Examples include:

- Linking to the official Vyact repository, releases, or website.
- Writing a review, tutorial, news article, or comparison about Vyact.
- Stating that your product or service is compatible with Vyact, provided the statement is accurate and does not imply endorsement.
- Identifying that your project is based on, derived from, or interoperates with Vyact.

Use only what is reasonably necessary for identification, and do not make the Vyact Marks more prominent than your own name or branding.

## Forks, modified versions, and services

You may fork, modify, and redistribute Vyact under the AGPL-3.0. If you do, you must use a clearly different name and visual identity for your version.

You may describe your version as “based on Vyact” or “a fork of Vyact”, but you may not use the Vyact Marks in a way that suggests it is the official Vyact project, an official release, or endorsed by Vyact.

In particular, do not:

- Name a modified version, hosted service, application, extension, or social-media account “Vyact” or a confusingly similar name.
- Use the Vyact logo or official brand assets as the primary identity of a modified version or service.
- Use the Vyact Marks in donation, sponsorship, crowdfunding, or payment pages in a way that could make people believe their support goes to the official Vyact project.
- Claim or imply sponsorship, affiliation, certification, or endorsement by Vyact without written permission.

## Permission requests

Uses outside this policy require prior written permission from the Vyact project maintainer. Please open a GitHub issue describing the proposed use, including where the name or logo will appear.

This policy does not transfer ownership of the Vyact Marks or grant a trademark license beyond the limited referential use described above. The policy may be updated as the project grows.
